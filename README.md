## Project Overview

**THE HUNT** is an AI-assisted cinematic short film set in a grounded early-15th-century European world.

The story follows three defeated soldiers escaping through a cold, rain-soaked forest, where they encounter an unknown armored figure silently approaching through the fog.

Rather than generating each shot independently, the project was developed through a structured filmmaking workflow — from script and shot design to 3D world blocking, camera blocking and full-film previsualization in Blender before generative video production.

The project explores how **3D previs and generative AI can work together to improve spatial continuity, character consistency, cinematography and directorial control.**

The film was developed as a four-act pursuit narrative:

## 3D World Blocking

Before camera design, the film was constructed as a unified spatial environment rather than a collection of independent shots.

A **90 × 110 m World Master Scene** was created to establish consistent geography, character positions, movement paths, combat zones and encounter distances across the entire film.

### Spatial System

- **90 × 110 m** forest environment
- **313** tree proxies
- **20 × 28 m** central encounter area
- Continuous movement paths for all four characters
- **15 m** final confrontation distance between Character A and the Knight
- **41.36 m** final escape route into the fog

## Blender Previsualization

The World Master Scene and 24-shot camera plan were then brought into **Blender** to build a full-film previsualization.

Rather than using 3D renders as final imagery, the previs was used as a structural reference for **composition, camera movement, character movement, timing and spatial continuity** before generative video production.

## Generative Production

With the visual bible, spatial blocking and camera previs established, the project moved into generative production.

Rather than generating isolated shots from text prompts alone, each shot was developed through a structured workflow combining **character references, environment references, shot composition, previs guidance and iterative generation**.

### Production Board

![Generative Production Board](04_PREVIS/04_GENERATIVE_WORKFLOW/Generative_Production_Board.jpg)

The production board served as a visual control system connecting character, environment, composition and shot-generation references across the film.

This workflow allowed generative tools to operate within an established directorial framework instead of determining the film's structure independently.
