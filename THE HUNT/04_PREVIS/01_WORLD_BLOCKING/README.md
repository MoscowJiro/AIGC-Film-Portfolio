## 3D World Blocking

Before camera design and generative production, the film was first constructed as a unified 3D spatial environment.

Instead of treating each shot as an independent generated scene, a **90 × 110 m World Master Scene** was created to establish consistent geography, character positions, movement paths and encounter distances across the entire film.

![World Master Scene](assets/previs/world-top-view.jpg)

### Spatial Design

The master scene established:

- **90 × 110 m** forest environment
- **1 unit = 1 meter**
- **313** mature tree proxies
- A central **20 × 28 m** encounter area
- Defined movement paths for A, B, C and The Knight
- Dedicated combat zones for C and B
- Camera-space reservations for later cinematography
- A **41.36 m** final escape route into dense fog

### Narrative Geography

A key constraint was that the geography had to explain the story.

During ACT III, **A remains at his original position** while C and B independently leave him to confront The Knight.

This allows ACT IV to begin naturally with:

**A ↔ The Knight: 15 m**

The distance is produced by character movement rather than resetting or teleporting characters between shots.

![Core Blocking](assets/previs/world-top-view-detail.jpg)

### Movement Logic

The spatial paths were defined before camera blocking:

**Escape → Clearing → Knight Approach → C Engagement → B Engagement → Final Confrontation → A Escape**

This master scene became the spatial reference for the following camera-blocking and Blender previs stages.
